About LeBron

LeBron Raymone James Sr. was born on December 30, 1984, in Akron, Ohio. He entered the NBA straight out of high school as the #1 overall pick in the 2003 draft and went on to redefine what a modern basketball player could be — a do-it-all forward who can score, rebound, pass, and defend at an elite level. Off the court, he's known for his business ventures, philanthropy (including the "I PROMISE School" in his hometown of Akron), and his outspoken advocacy on social issues.

Interesting Facts

  • LeBron is the NBA's all-time leading scorer, passing Kareem Abdul-Jabbar's record in February 2023.
  • He's the first player in NBA history to reach 40,000 career points.
  • He played alongside his son Bronny James on the Lakers in 2024 — the first father-son duo to play together in NBA history.
  • LeBron won an Olympic gold medal with Team USA in 2008, 2012, and 2016.
  • He was nicknamed "King James" while still in high school, where his games were nationally televised.
  • LeBron has never missed the playoffs in a full 82-game season for most of his career — a mark of his elite durability.
