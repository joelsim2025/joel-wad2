Biography

I'm Joel, and I study Information Systems at Singapore Management University. It's a course that sits right between the technical and the business side of things, which suits me — I like understanding how a system works as much as I like arguing about whether it should exist in the first place.

Most of my free time goes to sport. I follow football and basketball closely enough that my sleep schedule takes the blame for it, and I've been playing ultimate frisbee since I was 14 — long enough that it's stopped being a hobby and started being a habit. I support Chelsea, and I've watched LeBron for as long as I've watched basketball. Seeing either of them play live is the dream I'm still saving up for.

The rest of my time runs on coffee. I drink far more of it than anyone should, and I'm always happy to be pointed towards a good cafe. Outside of that you'll usually find me hunting for food around Singapore or planning a trip I haven't booked yet.

— Joel
